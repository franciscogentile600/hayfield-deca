About DECA

Business skills for the real world.

DECA is a competitive business club that prepares students and emerging leaders for careers in marketing, finance, hospitality, entrepreneurship, and business management. Those involved in DECA can participate in business-related challenges, learning experiences, and competitions at regional, state, and international levels.

Competition information

More than one way to compete.

For Individual Series Events and Team Decision Making Events, competition is comprised of two sections: a roleplay and an exam. The roleplay is a case study presented in front of a judge. Competitors receive an event situation and presentation outline and have a short period of time (10–30 minutes) to prepare a solution.

Prepared events involve a presentation accompanied by a written paper and/or slide deck. They can be completed individually or in a group of two to three people. Some prepared events also include an exam.

The competition experience

Bring your ideas. Make your case.

Practice the presentation, understand the indicators, and be ready to explain your solution.

Career clusters

Explore the five areas.

Start with a career area that interests you, then use the official DECA competitive event list to compare specific events, formats, and requirements.

Business Management & Administration

Explore the Business Management & Administration cluster and compare its individual, team, and prepared event options in the official event list.

Marketing

Marketing is all about turning creativity into cash flow! You’ll learn how to influence consumers, analyze market trends, implement promotional plans, and create advertising campaigns. Roleplays and exams center around skills in communication, promotion strategies, and brand management.

Finance

Finance helps you master the art of managing money! Focused on banking, investments, and budgeting, this cluster sharpens your analytical skills, attention to detail, and ethical decision-making. You might tackle roleplays and exams involving accounting principles, investment evaluations, or balancing company budgets.

Hospitality & Tourism

Hospitality and Tourism trains interpersonal skills and innovative problem solving! Focusing on hotels and travel, this cluster requires a creative mind, prioritizing customer service, and the ability to pitch an event. In H&T, roleplays and events could relate to consulting for a travel agency, dealing with an angry customer, or introducing new menu items.

Entrepreneurship

Explore the Entrepreneurship cluster and compare event options such as business plans, start-up concepts, and other entrepreneurship-focused challenges in the official DECA event catalog.

New to DECA? Take a look at Principles events.

If competing against experienced members sounds intimidating, a Principles event can be a great starting point. The chapter guide describes Principles events as introductory options across the five clusters for first-year members.
